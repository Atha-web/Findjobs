Agents at Work

A job-application assistant made of small AI agents. One finds and scores jobs, one writes applications, one keeps track of replies, and a scheduler keeps them on time. Here they are as robots in an office: watch them walk to the filing cabinets, hand each other envelopes and send an application.

The office

Each robot sits at its own desk. When it saves to the tracker it walks to the records cabinets, and emails travel through the mailroom and down the tube to the Employer inbox.

What's happening

    Who's who

    • SchedulerStarts the daily jobs on time.
    • ResearchFinds jobs, checks they're real and scores how well they fit.
    • ApplicationWrites the application and prepares the email.
    • TrackingUnderstands your replies and keeps every job's status up to date.
    • Telegram listenerReceives your messages and passes them to the right desk.
    • NotifierSends you updates and approval requests.
    • MailerHolds emails until you approve them, then sends them.
